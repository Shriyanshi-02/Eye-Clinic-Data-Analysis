\# Eye Clinic Data Analysis

\### Optimizing Pricing, Appointments, and Inventory in an Eye Clinic



\## Project Overview



This project focuses on analyzing the business operations of \*\*City Optics and Eye Care Centre\*\* to identify opportunities for improving pricing strategies, appointment management, and inventory planning.



The analysis was conducted as part of the Business Data Management (BDM) course at IIT Madras.



The objective was to use data-driven insights to understand operational challenges and recommend practical business improvements.



\## Problem Statement



The clinic faced several operational challenges:



\- Lens pricing compared with local competitors.

\- Appointment cancellations due to patient crowding.

\- Inventory management and stock optimization.

\- Identifying patterns in customer purchases and appointment timings.



\## Dataset Description



The project involved working with:



\- 306 patient records.

\- Sales and inventory information.

\- Pricing data collected from 15 local competitors.

\- Appointment and operational observations.



Data was collected through clinic records, direct observations, and staff interactions.



\*Note: Patient-identifiable and confidential business information should not be shared publicly. Any publicly distributed dataset must be appropriately anonymized and approved for sharing.\*



\## Tools and Technologies



\- \*\*Programming:\*\* Python

\- \*\*Libraries:\*\* Pandas, NumPy

\- \*\*Data Analysis:\*\* Statistical Analysis, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

\- \*\*Statistical Techniques:\*\* Pearson Correlation, Price Elasticity, Time-Series Analysis

\- \*\*Visualization:\*\* Matplotlib, Seaborn

\- \*\*Documentation:\*\* Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint



\## Methodology



1\. \*\*Data Collection:\*\* Collected patient, sales, inventory, and competitor pricing information.

2\. \*\*Data Cleaning:\*\* Standardized dates, handled missing values, and performed outlier analysis.

3\. \*\*Exploratory Data Analysis:\*\* Examined pricing patterns, appointment trends, and inventory distribution.

4\. \*\*Statistical Analysis:\*\* Applied descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, price elasticity, and time-series analysis.

5\. \*\*Visualization:\*\* Created charts and heatmaps to communicate findings.

6\. \*\*Recommendations:\*\* Developed practical suggestions to improve clinic operations.



\## Key Findings



\### 1. Pricing Analysis

\- The clinic's average lens price was ₹425.

\- The average competitor lens price was approximately ₹375.67.

\- The clinic's lens pricing was approximately \*\*13.1% higher\*\* than the competitor average.



\### 2. Appointment Analysis

\- Approximately \*\*23.5% of appointments\*\* were cancelled due to crowding (72 out of 306 records).

\- Higher cancellation activity was observed during weekday evening hours, particularly between 5 PM and 6 PM.



\### 3. Customer Purchase Patterns

\- Approximately \*\*87% of frame purchasers\*\* also purchased lenses.



\### 4. Inventory Analysis

\- The analysis identified potential overstocking in selected inventory categories.

\- Inventory planning and stock optimization were identified as areas for improvement.



\## Recommendations



Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were proposed:



\- Review lens pricing and consider competitive pricing strategies and product bundles.

\- Introduce better appointment scheduling and queue management.

\- Use appointment reminders to reduce missed appointments.

\- Improve inventory planning through demand-based stock monitoring.

\- Consider predictive ordering to reduce overstocking.
